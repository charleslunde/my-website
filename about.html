About me

Charles R. Lunde, infatuated with music from a young age, landed on the drum kit as his primary instrument. Currently he is a jazz and worship drummer, though he loves to work with all kinds of instruments. He writes his own music and has released two instrumental EPs on Spotify. From November 2022 to August 2025, he was the drummer for The Little Beasts, a small jazz combo (bass, piano, sax, drums) with a weekly gig at Little Beast Bistro. In July 2025, the group recorded a 5-track EP at Cue Recording Studios. With The Little Beasts, he performed at the Jim Ketch Jazz Festival in Durham, NC; the DC Jazz Festival; Arena Stage; Art All Night in Tenleytown; and the Jazz Education Network Conference, in both Atlanta (2025) and New Orleans (2026). The group also opened for the Billy Harper Big Band and the Music of Thelonious Monk Town Hall Orchestra at Georgetown Day School. He played in his high school jazz band all four years performing at the GDS Jazz Festival, Blues Alley, The Carlyle Room, and Pearl Street Warehouse. From March to August 2025 he taught one-on-one drum lessons, and in 2026 he played in the worship band at Westminster Woods summer camp.
